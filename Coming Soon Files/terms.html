02What the service does

You write a business idea in one short line. The site returns an automated score out of 100 and one short written assessment of that idea. Both are generated by an AI model against a published set of criteria.

Your submission, its score and its assessment are then published on a public page and ranked on one or both of two boards:

  • The Merit Board. Free. Every submission appears here, ordered by score alone. Placement on this board cannot be bought, influenced or altered by payment.
  • The Highlight Board. Paid. A separate board where position is determined by how much has been paid for the placement.

05The Highlight Board and what you are buying

A Highlight Board purchase buys one thing: a position in a public ranked list, held for as long as nobody pays more than you did. It is a promotional placement, not advertising inventory, not a subscription, and not a service delivered over time.

  • Placements are priced in United States dollars, in whole dollars, with a minimum of five dollars.
  • Increments are one dollar. To take the top position you must pay at least one dollar more than the current highest amount.
  • If you raise your own placement, you pay only the difference between your current amount and your new one.
  • Where two placements hold equal amounts, the one paid for earlier ranks higher.
  • Your position is claimed when payment completes, not when it is initiated.

Your placement is delivered immediately on payment. There is no minimum duration and no guaranteed length of time at any position. Others can and will outbid you, and when they do, you move down. That is the entire mechanic of the board and it is visible to everyone before they pay.

Amounts paid for every placement are displayed publicly alongside it.
